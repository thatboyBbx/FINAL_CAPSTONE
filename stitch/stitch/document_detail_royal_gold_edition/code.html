INSURANCE POLICY AGREEMENT - HARARE OFFICE

This Insurance Policy Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into this 14th day of October 2023 by and between Standard Bank Zimbabwe (the "Insurer") and Greenfield Agritech Ltd (the "Policyholder").

1. DEFINITIONS

"Policy Period" shall mean the term from January 1, 2024 to December 31, 2024. Note: This period overlaps with existing agricultural cycle protections.

2. PREMIUM REPAYMENT TERMS

The Total Premium payable for this coverage is USD 450,000.00 per annum. Failure to settle the premium within 30 days of the invoice date will result in immediate suspension of coverage. Risk: Immediate suspension clause violates the IPEC Grace Period directive (2019).
