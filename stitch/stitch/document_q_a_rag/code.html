What is the total liability cap for fire and explosion damage under section 4.2?
14:02 PM
auto_awesome
Based on Section 4.2 (Liability Aggregates) of the Commercial Property Policy, the total liability cap for fire and explosion is set at **USD 1,500,000** per occurrence [1]. This is subject to an annual aggregate limit of USD 5,000,000 across all insured premises listed in Schedule B [2].

Note: Claims arising from subterranean explosions are capped separately at 50% of the standard occurrence limit [3].
